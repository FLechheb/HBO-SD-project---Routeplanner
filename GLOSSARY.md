# Reisplanner

A public-transport journey planner that describes accessibility in terms of
traveller requirements rather than disabilities.

## Language

**Need**:
An independently selected traveller requirement, such as wheelchair access,
visual information, or extra transfer time. A traveller can combine several needs.
_Avoid_: Disability, diagnosis, person category

**Facility need**:
A traveller requirement concerning a facility used for movement or obtaining
information. It applies only to facilities whose role concerns that requirement.

**Timing need**:
A traveller requirement concerning journey timing, such as extra transfer time,
rather than the properties of an individual facility.

**Facility**:
An individual provision used for movement/navigation or obtaining information,
such as an elevator, staircase, information board, or speaker.

**Facility applicability**:
Whether a need concerns the role of a particular facility. A need that does not
apply is neither satisfied nor unsatisfied by that facility.

**Step-free access**:
Passage without stairs or steps, potentially using a ramp. It does not by itself
guarantee wheelchair accessibility or the presence of an elevator.
_Avoid_: Flat ground

**Guiding line**:
A tactile line that helps a traveller navigate.
_Avoid_: Guideline

**Extra transfer time**:
Additional time a traveller needs to change between journey legs. It is a timing
requirement, not a property that makes an individual facility accessible.
